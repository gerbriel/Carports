Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit for a metal carport or garage?

Permit requirements vary by county and city. Permits are the buyer's responsibility, but we provide all engineering drawings and documentation you'll need to submit to your local building department.

Can I customize the size and color?

Absolutely. Every structure we build is custom. You choose dimensions, roof style, wall panels, door/window placement, and color from our wide selection. Use our 3D builder to visualize before you commit.

What does the 10-year warranty cover?

Our warranty covers structural defects in materials and workmanship. We stand behind every build — if something fails due to our construction, we make it right.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We've partnered with HFS Financial to offer flexible financing options. You can get pre-qualified quickly with competitive rates and terms.

Who is responsible for site preparation?

Site prep (grading, clearing, foundation) is typically the buyer's responsibility. We can advise on what's needed and help coordinate, but final prep work is on your end before installation day.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Fresno and all of Northern California. Contact us to confirm service in your specific area — we work across a wide geographic region.
